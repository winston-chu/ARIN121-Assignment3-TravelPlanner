# ARIN121-Assignment3-TravelPlanner
This is an AI travel planning assistant that helps users create personalized travel itineraries and compare potential travel destinations based on their preferences, interests, and budget.

## Activities
### 1. Create a Travel Itinerary
This activity allows users to generate personalized day-to-day itineraries using the user's destination and trip duration, along with optional information such as budget, travel dates, interests, and preferences.
Each itinerary includes:
- Brief descriptions of suggested activities.
- Hotel recommendations based on budget and proximity to attractions.
- Local specialty dishes and recommended restaurants.
- Alternatives when an attraction or accommodation does not meet the user's requirements.

### 2. Compare Travel Destinations
This activity helps users decide between two or more travel destinations.
The assistant guides users through a multi-turn conversation by:
1. Asking which destinations they are considering.
2. Collecting their travel priorities, such as budget, food, or sightseeing.
3. Comparing destinations based on their preferences.
4. Recommending a destination and explaining the reasoning.
5. Asking for feedback and refining the recommendation based on the user's response.

## Activity Menu
When a user begins a conversation without clearly selecting an activity, the assistant displays a menu of the activity options
Users can select an activity by entering its number, writing its name, or describing their request in their own words.

## Switching Between Activities
Users can switch activities at any point during the conversation.
The assistant will switch to the destination comparison activity while retaining relevant information, including the user's budget, trip duration, destinations, and travel interests.
